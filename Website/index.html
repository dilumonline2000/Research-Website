§ 01 Abstract

One platform, four research problems.

Students currently juggle a reference manager, a search engine, a similarity checker, a mind-mapping app and a departmental spreadsheet, and none of them share data. Researchly AI argues that integrating these tools is itself the contribution.

Selected · ICDMIS 2026
Research Paper · R26-IT-116Dept. of Information Technology, SLIIT

AI-Powered Research Paper Assistant and Collaboration Platform for University Students

K D T Kariyawasam · S P U Gunathilake · N V Hewamanna · H W S S Jayasundara · Chandrasiri S. S. · L I E P Weerathunga

Undergraduate research is supported today by tools that were never designed to work together. Students format references in one application, search literature in a second, check similarity in a third, and are allocated supervisors through largely manual processes. The resulting fragmentation costs time, permits avoidable citation and integrity errors, and leaves institutions without a consolidated view of their own research output.

This paper presents the design and evaluation framework of an integrated, AI-powered research assistant and collaboration platform built from four modules behind a single interface. Module 1 provides citation generation in APA and IEEE styles, research gap analysis, retrieval-augmented proposal drafting and mind maps. Module 2 matches students to supervisors and peers using Sentence-BERT embeddings and a weighted multi-factor ranking, and classifies academic feedback. Module 3 performs multi-label topic categorisation, summarisation and plagiarism analysis. Module 4 forecasts emerging research domains and computes a four-dimensional weighted quality score. The platform contributes to SDG 4 and SDG 9.

Keywords — natural language processing, recommendation systems, research integrity, academic analytics, transformer models, higher education technology

§ 02 The four modules

Four components, one shared corpus.

Each module is an independent FastAPI service, but all of them read from the same embedded SLIIT corpus. A mind map can therefore show overlap data from Module 3, and Module 4 forecasts over the papers that Module 3 has categorised.

MODULE 01

Research Integrity & Compliance

A deterministic APA/IEEE citation engine with CrossRef lookup, evidence-backed research-gap analysis over 553 author-stated gap sentences, retrieval-grounded proposal drafting over 3,858 SLIIT exemplars, and concept mind maps.

  • Citation parser
  • Gap analysis
  • Proposal RAG
  • Mind maps
Kariyawasam K.D.T. · IT22210524
MODULE 02

Collaboration & Recommendation Engine

A Sentence-BERT matcher fine-tuned with contrastive loss ranks 74 SLIIT supervisors by similarity and availability, and explains each ranking. It also covers peer research groups, OTP-verified ratings with aspect-level sentiment, and transparent effectiveness scoring.

  • Supervisor matching
  • Peer connect
  • Feedback sentiment
Gunathilaka S.P.U. · IT22055194
MODULE 03

Research Data Collection & Management

Builds the shared 4,219 × 384 SBERT corpus index and audits its quality. It provides multi-label topic categorisation, faithful extractive summarisation, plagiarism-trend analysis and pairwise comparison that combines SBERT similarity with 4-gram Jaccard overlap.

  • Corpus index
  • Categoriser
  • Summariser
  • Overlap analysis
Hewamanne N.V. · IT22135902
MODULE 04

Research Performance Analytics & Visualization

Per-topic ARIMA forecasts with 95% prediction intervals, a four-dimension quality score that blends XGBoost regressors with calibrated heuristics, and success prediction that gives early warning on research topics.

  • Trend forecasting
  • Quality scoring
  • Success prediction
Jayasundara H.W.S.S. · IT22553096
§ 02.1 A student's journey

Describe an idea

The student enters a topic or a short proposal description.

Match supervisors

Module 2 ranks supervisors whose expertise fits and explains why.

Find the gap

Module 1 checks the topic against SLIIT gap statements, and Module 4 shows whether the area is growing.

Draft & cite

Module 1 drafts a proposal grounded in similar SLIIT papers and formats its references.

Check overlap

Module 3 compares the draft with closely related papers.

Score quality

Module 4 scores proposal quality and success probability so problems are caught early.
